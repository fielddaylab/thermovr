It is 1 (100%) when the "mixture" is all saturated vapor - we call this the saturated vapor line.---

In other words, in the two-phase region temperature and pressure are NOT independent.  If one of these properties changes, so does the other.  If one remains constant, so does the other.

When energy is added to the two-state system, the energy goes towards overcoming the heat of vaporization (increasing the quality and volume).---
